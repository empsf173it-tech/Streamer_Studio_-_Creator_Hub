My Editing Workflow

How I take your raw recordings and transform them into polished, broadcast-quality audio files.

Phase 1

Sound Calibration

Calibrating your microphones, setting levels, and optimizing local room acoustics.

Phase 2

Dialogue Editing

Surgical removal of filler words, long pauses, awkward pacing, and background noises.

Phase 3

Mastering & EQ

Applying dynamic EQ, compression, and loudness normalization to broadcast LUFS standards.

Phase 4

Sound Design

Seamlessly weaving in intro/outro music, transitions, sound effects, and sponsored messages.

Phase 5

SEO Show Notes

Writing keyword-rich descriptions, guest bios, links, and detailed timestamped chapters.

Phase 6

Hosting & Release

Scheduling episodes on your hosting platform and publishing to Apple, Spotify, and more.
