Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly do your production packages include?
My packages typically include professional audio editing, noise reduction, dynamic compression, ID3 tagging, drafting SEO-optimized show notes, and scheduling the episode across all major distribution platforms. Custom packages can also include video editing and social media clips.

How long does it take to edit an episode?
Standard turnaround time is 48-72 hours from the moment I receive your raw audio files. Expedited 24-hour turnaround is available upon request for an additional fee.

Do you help with podcast launch strategy?
Yes! I offer a comprehensive Podcast Launch package that covers equipment selection, show format planning, intro/outro creation, RSS feed setup, and submission to Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and other directories.

What if I only need consulting and not full production?
I offer 1-on-1 strategy calls and consulting sessions to help you optimize your recording setup, plan your content, and navigate the podcasting landscape without committing to a full production package.

Do you help with video podcasts as well?
Absolutely! I can edit your multicam video podcasts, create short-form clips for TikTok/Reels/Shorts, and publish the full video episodes on YouTube alongside the standard audio distribution.
